Soft Tissue–Suture RVE Homogenization

Computational investigation of the effective mechanical response of soft tissue reinforced by polymeric suspension sutures.

OBJECTIVE

From detailed geometry to effective mechanical behavior

The project develops representative volume element (RVE) models of a soft tissue–suture composite and evaluates its effective mechanical response under different loading conditions.

ADD RVE GEOMETRY / ABAQUS CONTOUR IMAGE

METHODS

  • Abaqus/Standard nonlinear finite element analysis
  • Hyperelastic soft-tissue matrix using a Yeoh material model
  • Polypropylene suspension suture reinforcement
  • Representative volume element modeling
  • Python-based model generation and post-processing
  • Effective stiffness evaluation from volume-averaged response
  • Mesh sensitivity and boundary-condition studies
